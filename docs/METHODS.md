# Methods

The canonical detailed method, assumptions and primary-source references are tracked in [public/methods.html](../public/methods.html) and shipped at `/methods.html`. The observed source metadata and SHA-256 are in [public/terrain/metadata.json](../public/terrain/metadata.json).

All analytical coordinates are EPSG:27700 metres. No volume claims are supported by this source. Four-neighbour seeded threshold connectivity, without a virtual barrier, yields potential connected low-ground area only. Ranking is by the minimum off-channel connected area across relative water/terrain elevation stress tests. Gradient and shape metrics are descriptive components displayed beside ranking, not uncalibrated weighted scores.
